Every token you ever bought opened an account that holds a little SOL. When the token dies, the SOL stays stuck in there. Autopsy burns the dead tokens, closes their accounts, and the SOL flows back to you.

  • You sign in your own wallet: 1 to 3 signatures, even for hundreds of bodies.
  • The SOL goes straight to your wallet. Autopsy never holds your funds.
  • A live gauge shows every account closing, and the flask filling up.
  • A small fee on the SOL recovered. $AUTOPSY holders bury for free.
